About

I am a Lecturer at the Faculty of Automatic Control and Computer Engineering, Technical University "Gheorghe Asachi" of Iași (TUIASI).

My professional and research activities are focused on control systems, electric machines and drives, automotive systems, parameter estimation and autonomous robotic systems.

I am also involved in teaching, research projects and the supervision of undergraduate and master's students.

Research

My research interests include modeling, estimation and control of dynamic systems, with applications in electric mobility, automotive systems and robotics.

Electric Drives

Modeling, identification and control of electric machines, including PMSM and SynRM drives.

Parameter Estimation

Online parameter estimation, Dynamic Regressor Extension and Mixing (DREM), observers and adaptive estimation.

Automotive Systems

Control and monitoring of automotive electric systems, embedded systems and electric powertrains.

Autonomous Robotics

Model predictive control, autonomous mobile robots and intelligent robotic platforms.
